About MOKI Beauty

Training That
Transforms Your Salon

We are a mobile Russian manicure education service, bringing expert-level training directly to nail salons. Our hands-on approach ensures every technician walks away with real, applicable skills.

Russian manicure (also known as the dry manicure technique) delivers impeccably clean cuticles and a longer-lasting service — and it's quickly becoming the most in-demand technique clients are requesting.
